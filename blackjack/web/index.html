EV is expected net profit per unit on the active hand, after a clear dealer check. Split EV includes both new hands; after splitting, recommendations apply only to the active hand. Double EV includes the extra wager.

Baseline EV is exact for infinite decks. Counting estimates are approximate: Hi-Lo uses stationary probabilities; other methods use first-order effects. They can be less accurate in depleted shoes. A sampled payout is not an EV.

Counts follow visible cards across rounds; hidden cards never enter recommendations. Entered cards condition the unseen shoe and dealer on that observation. The dealer completes automatically. Shallow reshuffle settings are experimental approximations, not calibrated Macau machine behavior.
